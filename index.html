Pulse X is Zgharta's gym for strength, conditioning and everyday fitness. Whether you are starting out or chasing a PR, you will find the space and the kit to do it properly.

Facilities

Everything on one level, laid out so you can move from warm up to lift to cooldown without wasting a minute.

Main Gym Floor

Racks, benches, plate loaded and selectorised machines, dumbbells and cardio, all in a wide open hall with natural light along two full glass walls.

Functional Studio

A separate hall for group classes, functional circuits, mobility and personal training sessions away from the main floor.

Shake & Supplement Bar

Protein shakes, drinks and supplements right at the entrance so you can refuel before you leave.

Changing Rooms

Separate men's and women's changing rooms and bathrooms, with secure gate access to the training floor.
